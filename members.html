Members

The three friends behind Pink Lady Slipper.

Abigail Wagner

Vocals

Abigail Wagner was born and raised in Crozet, Virginia, and hasn't shut up since. Earliest memories include singing The Chicks around her Oma's piano with her four sisters and mother. She can be found haunting local karaoke nights, and recently joined XOAP as accompaniment.

Ben Smith

Drums

From a microscope, Ben waved back from a Petrie dish to his parents and smiled for a picture. Nine months later, air would enter his lungs and he took in the colors and sounds of life. Music would find him from the back seat of an old Volvo on a daily basis. Sticks in hand, he would destroy his family's nice pillows until he awoke one Christmas morning at 7 years old to a green drum kit under the tree. A dream would come true over the next 21 years, looking at drums and cymbals from a throne filling rooms both small and big with noise, in hopes of hearing some noise back from the folks listening. Music lead him on adventures with people who would become great friends, spending a decade with rock outfit the BLNDRS. Now refusing to stop living a life of music and song, Pink Lady Slipper would find its pedals rising from the ground.

Eric Cope

Guitar

Eric was born in the back of a 1981 Pontiac Sunbird at the crack of midnight on Lover's Lane in Portage, Michigan. It was all downhill from there. Eric obsessively seeks out new music, but has a special place in his heart for Steely Dan and Grateful Dead. He previously helmed Charlottesville staples Tequila Mockingbird, Funktional Electrik, and Day of the Dead Allstars, as well as having stints in other local projects, such as Yonderphonics. Eric's goal is to create a better space for people using shared connection through music.

About

Pink Lady Slipper blends psychedelic rock, funk, blues, and jazz into high-energy, improvisation-heavy live shows. Powerful, soulful vocals, thundering rhythms, and searing leads combine into a truly exciting and original sound.

Based in Charlottesville, Virginia, Pink Lady Slipper is the brainchild of Eric Cope, Ben Smith, and Abigail Wagner, and the three's decades-long friendships shine through in the music. The band has taken thrilled fans around Virginia on a genre-defying sonic journey, and is emerging as one of Virginia's most exciting new bands.
